# Hanasu AI OS — Future Roadmap & Post-Phase 13 Horizons

Phase 13 successfully delivers the complete, self-contained single-user **Japanese Speaking AI Training OS** across voice immersion, conversation intelligence, memory, pronunciation acoustics, adaptive learning, YouTube shadowing, RPG gamification, and AI personal coaching.

The following represent potential future horizons for subsequent post-roadmap evolution:

---

## Future Horizons (Post-Phase 13)

### Horizon 1: Real-time Multi-Modal Streaming & Full Duplex Audio
- WebRTC direct bidirectional audio streaming with sub-100ms ultra-low latency voice interruption.
- Simultaneous real-time phoneme pitch comparison as the user speaks.

### Horizon 2: Native Mobile & Wearable Ecosystem
- React Native / Expo companion app for on-the-go commute shadowing and audio drills.
- Push notification reminders for daily streak protection and spaced-repetition reviews.

### Horizon 3: Multi-User Cloud Federation & Multiplayer Dojo
- Multi-tenant cloud deployment with OAuth2 / social login.
- Cooperative Boss Battles: Team conversation simulations (e.g. 2 learners + 1 AI business interviewer).
- Global learner leaderboards and guild quests.

### Horizon 4: Multi-Language Immersion Support
- Abstracting the Japanese pedagogical core to support Mandarin Chinese (Tones & Pinyin) and Korean (Hangul & Honorifics).
