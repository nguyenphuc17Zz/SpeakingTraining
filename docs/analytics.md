# Analytics & Personal AI Coach

## 1. Analytics Engine
- **Radar Metrics**: 6-dimension spoken Japanese evaluation (Grammar, Vocabulary, Pronunciation, Fluency, Naturalness, Engagement).
- **Session Velocity**: Measures response latency trends and speaking duration.
- **Weekly Review Engine**: Summarizes total speaking time, top mastered items, persistent roadblocks, and JLPT readiness projections.

---

## 2. Personal AI Coach
- **Grounded Conversational Intelligence**: The AI Coach reads live learner memory, recurring weaknesses, and exercise history to provide transparent, explainable recommendations.
- **Practice Now Integration**: Recommendations include a direct *"Practice Now"* action that launches targeted exercises inside the Learning Engine without duplicate logic.
